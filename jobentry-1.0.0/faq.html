Everything You Need to Know

Click on Register in the top menu or visit our Register page. Fill in your full name, email address, and a password (minimum 6 characters). Choose whether you're a job seeker or employer, agree to the terms, and click "Create Account". You'll receive a confirmation email (coming soon) and can start using HireHub immediately.

On the Login page, click the "Forgot Password?" link. Enter the email address associated with your account, and we'll send you a reset link. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

Absolutely! Once logged in, go to your Profile page and click the "Edit Profile" button. You can update your name, profile picture, and password. Your email address and account type cannot be changed for security reasons.

Yes! HireHub is completely free for job seekers. Employers can post jobs for free as well (limited to a certain number per month). We believe in making career connections accessible to everyone. Premium features may be added in the future, but the core functionality will always be free.

Use the search bar on the homepage or go to the Job List page. You can filter by keywords, category, location, and job type (Full‑Time, Part‑Time, Remote, Contract). Browse through the results and click on any job to view details and apply.

Yes! On each job card or detail page, click the heart icon (❤️) to save the job. Your saved jobs will appear in your profile under "Saved Jobs". You can view them anytime and apply when you're ready.

Click the "Apply Now" button on the job detail page. You'll be asked to fill in a short form with your contact details, upload your resume, and optionally include a cover letter. Once submitted, your application will be sent directly to the employer.

The Resume Analyzer is a free tool that reviews your uploaded resume (PDF, DOCX, or TXT) and gives you instant feedback. It checks for relevant skills, experience level, education, and provides personalized suggestions to improve your chances of getting hired.

Absolutely! The Resume Analyzer runs entirely in your browser – your file is never uploaded to any server. All processing is done client‑side using JavaScript. Your data stays private and secure on your own device.

After logging in as an employer, click the Post A Job button in the top menu. Fill in the job title, company name, job type, location, description, required skills, and application deadline. Once submitted, your job will appear in the job listings for candidates to discover and apply.

Yes, you can manage your job postings from your employer dashboard (coming soon). For now, please contact our support team if you need to modify or remove a listing.

We take privacy seriously. All passwords are hashed using bcrypt, and we use industry‑standard encryption (HTTPS) to secure data transmission. We never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Yes, we use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We don't use tracking cookies for advertising. You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings.

Currently, HireHub is free for all users. If we introduce paid features in the future, we'll support major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and other popular payment gateways.

Since all services are currently free, there are no refunds to process. If we introduce paid services in the future, we'll have a clear refund policy posted on this page.

You can reach us via the Contact page. Fill in your name, email, subject, and message, and we'll get back to you within 24–48 hours. For urgent issues, you can also email us directly at support@hirehub.com.

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (EST). We aim to respond to all queries within one business day. For self‑help, check our comprehensive FAQ page – you might find your answer right here!

Absolutely! We love hearing from our users. Use the Contact page to send us your suggestions, and we'll do our best to incorporate them into future updates.

Currently, HireHub is available in English. We plan to support additional languages in the future based on user demand. Stay tuned!

Yes! Our website is fully responsive and works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. You can browse jobs, apply, and manage your account from any device.
